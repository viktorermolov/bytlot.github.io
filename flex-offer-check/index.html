Plain answers

Does it record or send my screen anywhere?

No. Reading happens on your phone. That’s the whole point — no uploads, no per-scan fees, nothing to leak.

Will it drain my battery?

It does run in the background — that’s the only way to catch the 1-minute accept window. But it’s event-driven: it idles until an offer appears, reads the screen once, and shows the verdict in a notification. No continuous screen recording, nothing uploaded.

How much will it cost?

Early access is free. If it earns its keep, the plan is a small freemium — a few free checks a day, then a couple dollars a month. No price until it proves itself.
