# Phone Cost — candidate product

Phone Cost would compare the real multi-year cost of mobile service rather than advertised monthly pricing. Likely variables include lines, plan price, taxes/fees, devices and financing, trade-ins, credits, discounts, additional devices, incentives, and time horizon.

Useful outputs could include 12-, 24-, 36-, and 60-month cost, cost per line, option differences, and actual savings. This is not approved implementation work. Reassess demand, data needs, and calculation transparency after Driver Profit has evidence.
