# EV Cost — candidate product

EV Cost would reveal actual driving or ownership cost beyond electricity alone. Candidate inputs include electricity price, efficiency, charging losses, home/public charging mix, mileage, maintenance, tires, depreciation, and a gasoline comparison.

Candidate outputs include cost per mile, monthly operating cost, trip cost, EV-versus-gasoline difference, and break-even analysis. It is not approved implementation work; prioritize only with fresh evidence and transparent assumptions.
